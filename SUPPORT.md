# Support

Where to take what you have, so it reaches the right place:

- **The catalog first:** [every profile][catalog], what it owns and what it
  asks, may answer it already.
- **A question**, or a profile to talk over: [Discussions][discussions].
- **A bug, or a profile to request:** [an issue][issue], whose form asks for
  what a maintainer needs.
- **A bug in devset itself**, the tool that applies the profiles:
  [devset's issues][devset].
- **A vulnerability:** never an issue. [Report it privately][advisory].

[catalog]: https://atomix-labs.github.io/atxp/
[discussions]: https://github.com/atomix-labs/atxp/discussions
[issue]: https://github.com/atomix-labs/atxp/issues/new/choose
[devset]: https://github.com/atomix-labs/devset/issues/new/choose
[advisory]: https://github.com/atomix-labs/atxp/security/advisories/new
