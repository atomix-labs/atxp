# Support

Where to take what you have, so it reaches the right place:

- **The catalog first:** [every profile](https://atomix-labs.github.io/atxp/),
  what it owns and what it asks, may answer it already.
- **A question**, or a profile to talk over:
  [Discussions](https://github.com/atomix-labs/atxp/discussions).
- **A bug in devset itself**, the tool that applies the profiles:
  [devset's issues](https://github.com/atomix-labs/devset/issues/new/choose).
- **A bug, or a feature to request:**
  [an issue](https://github.com/atomix-labs/atxp/issues/new/choose), whose form
  asks for what a maintainer needs.
- **A vulnerability:** never an issue.
  [Report it privately](https://github.com/atomix-labs/atxp/security/advisories/new).
